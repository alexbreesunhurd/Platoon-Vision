01The Moment

Platoon was built for a different era.

Founded 2016. Acquired in 2018. Today, three forces have changed what "artist services" should mean, and Platoon have the resources to adapt to all three faster than anyone else.

i.

AI collapses A&R

Anyone with a laptop can find a viral artist. Scouting on TikTok metrics is now table stakes - and a commodity.

ii.

Distribution is a race to zero

DistroKid, TuneCore, UnitedMasters. Margins compress. Differentiation has to come from what happens after upload.

iii.

Fans want ownership, not feeds

Substack, Patreon, Discord, group chats. The relationship between artist and fan is moving direct - and Apple owns the device.

06Pillar III · Direct-to-Fan

Apple owns the device. Build the commerce layer between artist and fan.

D2F is the dominant trend in artist economics: Patreon, Substack, Discord, group chats. Apple is the only company that can build it natively into 2bn+ devices via iMessage, Wallet, and Apple Pay. Platoon should be the artist services arm that ships it.

i.

The fan rail

Native fan inboxes via iMessage Business. Drops, presales, voice notes, ticket priority. Direct from artist, opt-in by fan.

ii.

Co-owned data

Apple privacy-first. Artist sees fan signal (city, listening, engagement). Fan stays anonymous. Platoon gets the aggregate layer.

iii.

Revenue share

Tiered partnership deals: distribution-only, services-plus, or full 360 with D2F. Platoon takes a cut of fan-driven revenue, not just streams.

The win Apple positions itself as the most artist-friendly home in music, and earns recurring revenue beyond streaming.
07Two Tiers, One OS

Curate at the top. Build a platform underneath.

A boutique-only label limits its own ambition. The same Artist OS that develops a curated cohort can serve thousands of independents, with technology doing the lifting humans can't scale to. Two distinct products, one tech stack, one feeder pipeline.

Tier 1 · The Cohort
~10 artists / year

Apple's prestige roster.

Hand-picked. Heavy human touch. Apple's full ecosystem behind every signing.

  • Bespoke 3-5 year plans mapped to TV+, Fitness+, Beats, Shazam, devices.
  • Significant capital behind each artist for production, touring, marketing.
  • Editorial & sync as a service: Platoon's Apple relationships are the moat.
Graduation ↑ Signal-driven
Tier 2 · The Platform
500+ artists / year, scaling to thousands

Tech-led artist development.

Open application. Same Artist OS. Algorithmic routing. Humans intervene only where the data justifies it.

  • Self-serve onboarding with the same OS the cohort uses.
  • Automated opportunity routing: sync briefs, playlist pitching, marketing spend triggered when traction crosses thresholds.
  • Artists who break through graduate up. The platform is the cohort's discovery engine.
One OS The same product powers both tiers. That's what makes the volume play economically sane and strategically coherent. The OS investment pays for itself twice.
