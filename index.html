05Pillar III · Direct-to-Fan

Platoon builds the commerce layer between artist and fan, on the devices Apple already owns.

D2F is the dominant trend in artist economics: Patreon, Substack, Discord, group chats. No artist services company can ship it natively into 2bn+ devices. Platoon can, by building on iMessage, Wallet, and Apple Pay. This is how Platoon becomes the rail every artist wants to be on.

i.

The fan rail

Native fan inboxes via iMessage Business. Drops, presales, voice notes, ticket priority. Direct from artist, opt-in by fan. Owned by Platoon, sat on rails Apple controls.

ii.

Co-owned data

Apple-grade privacy. Artist sees fan signal (city, listening, engagement). Fan stays anonymous. Platoon owns the aggregate layer, the only company in music with a clean view across the rail.

iii.

Revenue share

Tiered partnership deals: distribution-only, services-plus, or full 360 with D2F. Platoon takes a cut of fan-driven revenue, not just streams. The economics of a label, the margin profile of a platform.

What opt-in unlocks

Once a fan opts in, every Apple surface becomes a Platoon-orchestrated touchpoint.

The fan rail is the consent layer. Once the fan has opted in via iMessage Business, Platoon's OS orchestrates the rest of Apple's surfaces around them, with their permission, never beyond it.

Maps Fan walks past a venue, gets a notification: their artist is playing here tomorrow. Triggered by listening history + Shazam, opt-in only.
Calendar Tour dates, release days, livestream premieres auto-populate the fan's calendar. With reminders. With one-tap ticket links.
Apple News Artist's interviews, features, long-reads surface in News for opted-in fans. Editorial and commerce in the same feed.
Safari Drop pages, fan portals, and ticket flows checkout via Apple Pay (no forms, Face ID confirms). Tickets and membership credentials drop straight into Wallet. The fan is recognised as opted-in, the artist site greets them by tier, all without a single login screen.
The win Platoon becomes the most artist-friendly home in music, and earns recurring revenue beyond streaming. Apple gets the only artist services company building natively for its ecosystem.

07Two Tiers, One OS

Curate at the top. Scale the discovery underneath.

A small, deeply-backed cohort at the top, signed by humans. A larger platform underneath, fed by two parallel routes: artist submission, and an internal discovery tool. Same criteria gate both. Same Artist OS powers both tiers. Two distinct products, one standard.

Tier 1 · The Cohort
10 artists / year. Held lean by design.

Apple's prestige roster.

Hand-picked content-savvy artist-creators. Heavy human touch. Apple's full ecosystem behind every signing. The team can buy in fully because the bar is genuinely high.

  • Bespoke 3-5 year plans mapped to TV+, Fitness+, Beats, Shazam, devices.
  • Significant capital behind each artist for production, touring, marketing.
  • Editorial & sync as a service: Platoon's Apple relationships are the moat.
Graduation ↑ Signal-driven
Tier 2 · The Platform
Two routes in. One curation bar.

Tech-led A&R, at scale.

Away from the cohort, the A&R discovery piece is scalable. Two parallel feeders bring artists in: a submission portal where artists put themselves forward, and a proprietary discovery tool the A&R team uses internally. Both are gated by the same criteria.

  • The Platform (artist-facing): a public submission portal. Artists apply directly. Criteria-gated, not open self-serve.
  • The discovery tool (internal): A&R team's instrument for surfacing emerging artists across streaming, socials, Shazam, live data, and scenes humans aren't watching.
  • Core criteria as the curation layer: a defined bar (creative quality, content fluency, momentum) gates entry from either route.
  • Standout Platform artists graduate to the Cohort. The Platform is the proving ground.
One Standard Same OS, same bar, both tiers. Apple doesn't ship anything mediocre. Neither should the label inside it. The Cohort is curated by people. The Platform is curated by criteria. Both are curated.
